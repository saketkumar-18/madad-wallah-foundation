Our Programmes

What We Run on the Ground

Each programme is designed around our registered objectives and delivered with local volunteers and partners.

Education

Educational Support Drives & Literacy Campaigns

Distribution of books, stationery and learning material, evening study support and adult literacy campaigns in rural Samastipur.

Healthcare

Free Medical & Health Check-up Camps

Periodic health camps with basic check-ups, medicine distribution, and awareness sessions on hygiene and family welfare.

Relief

Food, Clothing & Essential Distribution

Ration kits, warm clothing and daily essentials delivered to families facing hardship and seasonal distress.

Environment

Tree Plantation & Protection Drives

Community plantation events, sapling care drives and awareness on water conservation and sanitation.

Empowerment

Women Skill Development Workshops

Self-reliance workshops covering tailoring, handicrafts, financial literacy and small-enterprise skills for women.

Want to start a programme in your area?

Partner with us as a volunteer or community organiser and bring these programmes to your village or ward.
