Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Home / Privacy Policy

MADAD WALLAH FOUNDATION ("we", "our", "the Foundation") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Registration details — when you submit the volunteer/member/donor form (via Google Forms or the on-site form): full name, mobile number, email, date of birth, state & district, educational qualification, role applied for, and preferred sector of interest.
  • Donation details — information you share while making a donation (name, contact details, payment reference). Bank/UPI payments are processed by your bank or UPI app; we do not store card or UPI PIN data.
  • Correspondence — contents of emails or messages you send us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To coordinate volunteering, membership and donor relations.
  • To issue donation acknowledgements and receipts.
  • To comply with statutory and regulatory obligations under the Companies Act, 2013 and applicable laws.
  • To send programme updates you have opted into.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Data may be shared only: (a) with service providers such as Google Forms/Sheets used to collect registrations; (b) where required by law or a government authority.

4. Data Retention & Security

We retain registration and donation records for the period required for statutory compliance and programme management, and apply reasonable security measures to protect them.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by writing to madadwallahfoundation@gmail.com.

6. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies. Embedded third-party content (e.g., Google Maps, Google Forms, YouTube) may set their own cookies as per their policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
