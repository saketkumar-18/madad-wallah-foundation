Our Story

Rooted in Bihar, Serving India

MADAD WALLAH FOUNDATION operates under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, focusing on sustainable community growth, health awareness, literacy, and environmental protection across Bihar and India.

The word "Madad" means help — and that is exactly who we are: people who show up. From educational support drives in Samastipur to health camps and disaster relief, our work is powered by volunteers and guided by transparency.

Incorporated on 12 August 2026 and headquartered in Rosera, Samastipur (Bihar), the foundation works with a primary focus on the Samastipur region while extending its operations across the whole of India.

Vision & Mission

🔭

Our Vision

An India where every community — regardless of geography or circumstance — has access to quality education, healthcare, and dignity; where women lead, children learn, and no family faces a crisis alone.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower underserved communities through sustained programmes in education, health, women's skill development, environmental conservation and disaster relief — delivered with transparency, volunteer energy, and local partnership.
