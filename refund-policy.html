Refund & Cancellation Policy

For online donations · Last updated: August 2026

Home / Refund Policy

This policy applies to donations made to MADAD WALLAH FOUNDATION through bank transfer, UPI or any online payment channel.

1. Nature of Donations

All donations are voluntary, non-refundable contributions towards the Foundation's charitable objectives as registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013. Once a donation is received and acknowledged, it is generally not refundable.

2. Exceptions — When Refunds Are Considered

  • Duplicate payment: if the same donation is accidentally paid more than once, the duplicate amount will be refunded.
  • Erroneous payment: if a payment was made due to a demonstrable technical error (e.g., amount debited but not credited to the Foundation's account), we will verify with our bank and refund the amount.
  • Fraudulent/unauthorised transaction: claims supported by a bank or payment-provider dispute will be handled in coordination with the relevant institution.

3. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email madadwallahfoundation@gmail.com within 7 days of the transaction.
  2. Include: donor name, transaction reference/UTR, date, amount, and reason for the request.
  3. Our team will acknowledge the request within 3 working days and communicate the outcome after verification.

4. Processing

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment source within 7–14 working days, subject to banking timelines.

5. Cancellation of Recurring Commitments

If you have committed to a recurring contribution, you may cancel future instalments at any time by emailing us — no further amounts will be expected.
