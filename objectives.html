Six Domains of Service

Every programme we run falls within these six objective domains registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013.

📚

Education & Skill Development

  • Promoting literacy and educational aid
  • Youth empowerment programmes
  • Vocational & skill training
  • Support for underprivileged students
🏥

Health & Family Welfare

  • Organising free health camps
  • Hygiene & sanitation drives
  • HIV/AIDS awareness programmes
  • Healthcare distribution support
👩‍👧

Women & Child Empowerment

  • Child education support
  • Women safety awareness
  • Skill development for women
  • Elderly care initiatives
⚖️

Social Welfare & Legal Rights

  • Dalit upliftment programmes
  • Human rights awareness
  • Legal assistance & aid
  • Poverty alleviation initiatives
🌳

Environment & Water Management

  • Tree plantation drives
  • Environmental conservation
  • Clean drinking water access
  • Sanitation improvement
⛑️

Disaster Relief

  • Emergency food distribution
  • Clothing assistance
  • Shelter support during calamities
  • Rapid response coordination
